## Limitations

- **No backtest trade ledger.** `momentum_v1.0_trade_ledger.csv` holds the live AI paper account's 12 trades. Per-trade logs for the 16 backtest folds were never stored; producing them means re-running the folds on a lab seeded with the 1,000-stock pool, on the engine as it stands.
- **Ledger columns not stored:** gross P&L, fees and slippage are not recorded separately (the stored entry price is cost-loaded), and no strategy version is stored per trade. Left empty rather than re-derived.
- **Unfilled orders:** the AI run row records only bought and sold counts, and the buy pass records only executed trades, so orders that were not filled leave no row to export.
- **First paper cohort:** The first cohort of trades was placed on 2026-08-25 from signals generated at 10:18 IST off in-progress bars (audit finding #16). They stand as traded.
- **Precision:** backtest — experiment scripts round per-fold metrics to 2 decimals before writing results.json. Live — Figures as published by the API, which rounds to 2 decimals.
- **Benchmark:** NIFTY 50 price index (^NSEI via yfinance). A price index, not total return: it excludes dividends, so it understates what the benchmark actually returned.
- **Earlier phases** (11–22) have not been re-run with fixes #13, #18, #19 or #20. Only Phase 23 — the backtest evidence above — ran on the fixed engine.
